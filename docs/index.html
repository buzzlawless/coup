Coup Explorer

Heads-up, one influence each, nobody bluffs. Every move is shown with its exact win probability under perfect play. Pick any branch, for either side.

      With a side played automatically it takes the highest-EV move every time, and the deck is rolled rather than chosen — picking your own draw, or your opponent's, is the point of exploring but not of playing. Solved by value iteration over every legal position. EVs are the probability the player to move wins, assuming perfect play by both from here on. Upcards cannot change during a game, so only that slice of the table is loaded.
